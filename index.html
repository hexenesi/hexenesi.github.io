About

I'm a software developer drawn to the places where low-level systems meet mathematics. Fractals and strange attractors are what got me writing renderers in the first place, and that interest in tight loops and numerical behavior shows up in everything else I build.

Day to day that means optimizing C++, writing Python utilities, architecting Django backends, and designing frontends that stay out of the way. I care about efficiency, clarity, and code that is still legible a year later.

Lately most of my time goes to automation and agentic software pipelines, home automation integrations, and continued experiments with chaotic systems — much of it in private repositories while it matures.
